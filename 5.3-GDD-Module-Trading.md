# GDTLancer - Trading Module

**Version:** 3.6
**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Related Documents:** [1-GDD-Core-Mechanics.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/1-GDD-Core-Mechanics.md) (v5.5), [8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md) (v2.5)

## 1. Overview

Governs all economic activities: contract-based cargo transport, delivery, and salvage retrieval. Primary loop for progressing through Wealth Tiers and Tracks. Trading is a **Narrative Action** domain — outcomes depend on character skill, wealth tier modifiers, and dice, not real-time reflexes.

**Simulation Layer Mapping:** Contract fulfillment moves goods from origin station stockpiles to target station stockpiles (see [8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md) Section 3.6), modifying Grid-layer `commodity_stockpiles`. All cargo is physically conserved (**Axiom 1**): loading cargo moves units from station stockpile to agent cargo; delivery reverses the transfer to the target station. No matter is created or destroyed.

## 2. Core Mechanic: The Contracts and Cargo Interface

UI-based hub for managing active cargo contracts and viewing regional commodity demands.

* **Trigger:** Player docks at a market location.
* **Market Interface:** Menu displays available contracts at the station, player's active cargo/contracts, and wealth track impact. Direct market speculation (buying/selling arbitrary commodities on speculation) is disabled; players instead select and accept transport or retrieval jobs.
* **Pricing & Value:** Commodity base values are static. Factions generate contracts based on CA-driven regional demand and shortages. The value class of the contract (Low, Mid, High) is determined by the value class of the cargo and the difficulty of the route.
* **Economic Loop:** Accept cargo transit or salvage retrieval contracts. Deliver the specified goods to the destination sector to increment progress on the active **Wealth Track**. Transactions directly impact the player's qualitative **Wealth Tracks** (Broke, Comfortable, Wealthy) as virtual accounting adjustments (which have no physical cargo weight).
* **Contract Fulfillment Rules:**
  When a contract is successfully delivered, the impact on the player's active wealth track is determined by the Contract Value Class (Low, Mid, High) relative to the player's current Wealth Tier:
  - **Low-Value Contracts (e.g., raw ores, water transport):**
    - *Broke Tier:* Fulfilling increments track by +1 progress.
    - *Comfortable/Wealthy Tiers:* Low-value contracts are minor; they cause no change to wealth progress (+0).
  - **Mid-Value Contracts (e.g., consumer goods, basic components transport):**
    - *Broke Tier:* Fulfilling increments track by +2 progress.
    - *Comfortable Tier:* Fulfilling increments track by +1 progress.
    - *Wealthy Tier:* Mid-value contracts are minor; they cause no change to wealth progress (+0).
  - **High-Value Contracts (e.g., advanced electronics, heavy machinery transport):**
    - *Broke Tier:* Locked (player lacks credentials/reputation collateral).
    - *Comfortable Tier:* Fulfilling increments track by +2 progress.
    - *Wealthy Tier:* Fulfilling increments track by +1 progress.

## 3. Narrative Actions in Trading

Social and economic decisions resolved by dice rolls — implicit (auto-resolved, result as toast) or explicit (full dice UI), depending on Action Stakes.

* **Mechanic:** `3d6 + Module Modifier` Action Check.
* **Action Stakes:** Per action template.
* **Consequences:** Successful negotiation → improved Contact Relationship, or a wealth progress cost reduction of 1, or a wealth progress gain increase of 1. Failed deal → damaged Faction Standing and Reputation.

### Phase 1 Trading Actions
| Action | Stakes | Outcome |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Negotiate Bulk Deal | Narrative | Better contract payout (wealth impact modified by +1 progress) + Contact Relationship vs. worse payout + damaged relationship |
| Seek Rare Goods | Mundane | Discover high-value contract tip-off vs. time wasted |

## 4. Contracts System

Contracts drive the Phase 1 economic loop. Players accept contracts via station terminals, carry out the transport or salvage tasks, and receive qualitative wealth track progression upon delivery.

* **Contract Types:**
  - **Cargo Transit:** Source station loads specified commodity units into player cargo hold. Player transports them to target station.
  - **Salvage Retrieval:** Player is given coordinates of a wreck or debris field, retrieves specific cargo/commodities, and delivers them to the requesting station.
* **Contract Generation:** Generated dynamically from Grid-layer faction demand tags (`CONTRACT_DEMAND_*`).
* **Failure Penalty:** Failing to deliver contract cargo (e.g., cargo destroyed, contract expired) decrements the player's active wealth track progress by the contract's base value modifier and damages faction standing.

## 5. Required Phase 1 Stats

* **Agent:** `skill_trading` — base for Module Modifier.
* **Ship:** `cargo_capacity` — max commodity units (Virtual wealth progress does not occupy cargo space).
* **Commodity:** `item_id`, `name`, `base_value`, `value_class` (Low, Mid, High).
* **Systems:** Character (wealth_tier, wealth_progress, standings), Inventory (cargo), Asset (capacity), Time, CoreMechanicsAPI.
