# GDTLancer - Trading Module

**Version:** 3.1
**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Related Documents:** [1-GDD-Core-Mechanics.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/1-GDD-Core-Mechanics.md) (v5.0), [8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md) (v2.1)

## 1. Overview

Governs all economic activities: buying/selling commodities. Primary loop for accumulating wealth via electronic credits and physical specie. Trading is a **Narrative Action** domain — outcomes depend on character skill and dice, not real-time reflexes.

**Simulation Layer Mapping:** Trade actions modify Grid-layer `commodity_stockpiles` (see [8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md) Section 3.6). Prices use static base values with CA-driven modifiers applied on top (see [8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/8-GDD-Simulation-Architecture.md) Section 3.4). CA 2 (Supply & Demand Flow, [1.2-GDD-Core-Cellular-Automata.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/1.2-GDD-Core-Cellular-Automata.md)) propagates price changes across locations. All traded goods are physically conserved (**Axiom 1**): selling moves units from agent cargo to station stockpile; buying reverses the transfer. No matter is created or destroyed.

## 2. Core Mechanic: The Trade Interface

UI-based hub for market activity.

* **Trigger:** Player docks at a market location.
* **Market:** Menu displays player cargo, station inventory, buy/sell prices. Execute buy/sell orders.
* **Pricing:** Base commodity prices are static. CA-driven `commodity_price_deltas` modify prices per location based on local supply/demand conditions. Player trade actions feed back into the CA.
* **Economic Loop:** Buy low, sell high for net profit. Profits are accumulated as either trust-bound electronic credits or physical specie tokens:
  - **Electronic Credits:** Profile-bound virtual currency that has no cargo weight or footprint.
  - **Physical Specie:** Cargo commodity tokens representing physical refined metals. These occupy ship cargo capacity, presenting a hauling opportunity cost.
* **Trust-Gated Routing:** When a trade occurs, the transaction payment instrument is dynamically resolved based on faction trust ([REV_008](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/TRUTH_GDD-REVISION-LEDGER.md#L110)):
  - If the Faction Affinity between the parties is greater than or equal to `CREDIT_TRUST_THRESHOLD`, payment is processed in **Electronic Credits**.
  - If the Faction Affinity is below the threshold, or either party is factionless, the transaction resolves to **Physical Specie** unconditionally.
  - If specie is required but the buyer lacks sufficient cargo-space or inventory count of `commodity_specie`, the transaction is blocked.
* **Loyalty Points:** Faction-aligned trade (selling needed goods to your faction's station) earns LP. LP are finite, contribution-tracked, and spent on faction-exclusive services.

## 3. Narrative Actions in Trading

Social and economic decisions resolved by dice rolls — implicit (auto-resolved, result as toast) or explicit (full dice UI), depending on Action Stakes.

* **Mechanic:** `3d6 + Module Modifier` Action Check.
* **Action Stakes:** Per action template.
* **Consequences:** Successful negotiation → improved Contact Relationship. Failed deal → damaged Faction Standing and Reputation.

### Phase 1 Trading Actions
| Action | Stakes | Outcome |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Negotiate Bulk Deal | Narrative | Better price + Contact Relationship vs. worse price + damaged relationship |
| Seek Rare Goods | Mundane | Discover unlisted commodity tip-off vs. time wasted |

Contracts (delivery jobs, faction missions) are planned as an overlay system on top of the trade and agent infrastructure. Implementation deferred until the world and agent systems are operational. See [2.1-GDD-Development-Phase1-Scope.md](file:///home/roalyr/Software_archive/Games/GDTLancer-game-design/2.1-GDD-Development-Phase1-Scope.md) Phase 1 milestones.

## 5. Required Phase 1 Stats

* **Agent:** `skill_trading` — base for Module Modifier.
* **Ship:** `cargo_capacity` — max commodity units (Physical Specie occupies cargo space, credits do not).
* **Commodity:** `item_id`, `name`, `base_value`.
* **Systems:** Character (credits, standings, LP), Inventory (cargo, including `commodity_specie` tracking), Asset (capacity), Time, CoreMechanicsAPI.
